# Seedbed Quality Rubric

Score the draft using concrete evidence from the artifact. Do not award points for section presence alone.

| Criterion | Max | Full-credit evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Thesis and causality | 15 | A decisive thesis links root problem, consequence, value, and why now. |
| Grounding and evidence | 15 | Material claims trace to sources; hypotheses are explicit. |
| Clarity and no ambiguity | 15 | Terms, boundaries, dependencies, and actors have one operational meaning. |
| Observable outcomes | 15 | Success and false-success detection are falsifiable. |
| Invariants and failures | 10 | Laws cover empty, partial, degraded, invalid, and fatal states as applicable. |
| Decisions and tradeoffs | 10 | Closed decisions preserve rationale, rejected cost, and consequence. |
| Scope coherence | 10 | In/out boundaries align with thesis and contain no hidden prerequisite. |
| Executable handoff | 10 | Ordered workstreams, gates, and acceptance evidence require no product decision. |

Persistence requires at least 90/100, non-zero scores for thesis, grounding, clarity, outcomes, and handoff, and zero unresolved material contradictions. Revise weak criteria before asking more questions. Ask only when revision cannot supply missing user intent or evidence.

After writing, re-score the persisted artifact. Report criterion totals honestly; never inflate a score to cross the threshold.
